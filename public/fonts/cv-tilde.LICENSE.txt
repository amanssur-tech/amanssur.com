cv-tilde.ttf

A one-glyph typeface used to render the ASCII tilde (U+007E) in the CV.
The outline is derived from Syne (U+02DC) by Bonjour Monde, scaled to the proportions of
Trebuchet MS's tilde. Syne is licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, which permits
modification and redistribution provided the font is not sold on its own and this licence
accompanies it: https://openfontlicense.org
